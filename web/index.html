How it works
From complaint to accountability
in four steps
RastaNidhi traces every rupee from budget allocation to road repair — and flags when something doesn't add up.
1

Citizen reports an issue

File a complaint via app, helpline or walk-in. Claude classifies it, detects duplicates and links it to the right ward.

→
2

Work order is raised

BMC raises a repair work order linked to the complaint. Contractor, method, area and cost are all recorded.

→
3

AI audits the work

Claude checks the bill against the rate card, flags DLP violations, duplicate billing and repairs that failed within 30 days.

→
4

Public accountability

Citizens, officers and corporators see a plain-English report — who did what, what it cost and whether it held up.
